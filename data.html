COVID-19 medical announcements dataset

The hand-collected dataset behind Part I of the paper, released publicly. It has 15,839 announcements from 20 countries, 20 Jan 2020 – 28 Feb 2022, time-stamped to the minute in UTC.

ColumnDescription
timeMinute-level timestamp of the announcement, in UTC (e.g. 2020-03-14T19:35:00Z).
countryCountry code: AR, AU, BR, CA, CH, CL, CN, CN-HK, CO, DE, ES, FR, IN, IT, JA, KR, MX, NZ, UK, US.
categorycases (publication of case counts), live (live-streamed press conference), president speech (led by the President or Prime Minister), or other.
peak1 if the announcement came at the country's usual briefing time (regularly scheduled), 0 otherwise.

In the replication package, only the announcements file is the actual data. Newspaper tweet text and proprietary financial series (e.g. Bloomberg, Thomson Reuters, EPFR) are replaced by pseudo data because of provider sharing policies.
